About

This Way, Please is a New York based styling and communications practice for people and brands who care about how things are said, and how they show up.

We work in two directions: styling for individuals, and brand strategy and PR for startups and established businesses.

Led by Founder Nicole, This Way, Please is built on a sharp eye, strong editorial instincts, and a research-driven approach, honed across nearly a decade in PR and communications, from Nili Lotan to Alice + Olivia, The Brandman Agency, CARO PR, Éclat, and A Design Partnership, working across design, fashion, and hospitality brands.

That same eye for detail shows up in the styling work: closet edits, in-home and virtual consultations, and looks pulled together for vacation, everyday wear, office attire, or a big night out.

Styling
Closet edits, in-home and virtual styling consultations, and full look pulls for whatever the moment calls for. Every closet and every client is different, so every session is built around you.

This Way, Please PR
Brand strategy, go-to-market strategy, and media relations for startups and established businesses. Drawing on years of press relationships and event expertise across design, fashion, and hospitality, we help brands find their voice and their visibility.
